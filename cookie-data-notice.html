Mega Financial Cookie & Data Collection Notice

Effective Version: September 16, 2026

This notice explains the browser storage, technical information, and third-party technologies that may be involved when you use the Mega Financial website and protected client portal.

1. Browser Storage

The Mega Financial client portal may use browser storage to maintain information necessary for authenticated client and administrator sessions. This allows protected pages to recognize an authenticated session and provide the appropriate portal experience.

2. Authentication Information

Authentication information may be stored temporarily in the user's browser as part of the current client portal architecture. Users should log out when using a shared or public device and should not intentionally provide their authentication credentials or session information to another person.

3. Policy Notice Preference

Browser storage may be used to remember that a website visitor has dismissed or acknowledged the current Cookie & Data Collection notification. An authenticated client's formal policy acknowledgement is recorded separately by Mega Financial's protected server workflow.

4. Technical Information

Like other internet services, the website, hosting infrastructure, and service providers involved in delivering Mega Financial services may process technical request information necessary to deliver, secure, troubleshoot, and operate their services.

5. Third-Party Services

Mega Financial may use third-party providers for functions such as hosting, secure document storage, database services, payments, scheduling, email, and virtual meetings. Those providers may use cookies or similar technologies when their services are accessed and may maintain their own privacy and cookie policies.

6. Advertising and Marketing Cookies

The current Mega Financial application does not intentionally rely on advertising cookies to create advertising profiles through its own application code. If Mega Financial later introduces advertising, analytics, or other optional tracking technologies, this notice and the related user controls should be reviewed and updated before those technologies are enabled.

7. Managing Browser Data

Browsers generally provide controls for viewing or clearing cookies, site data, and browser storage. Clearing browser data may sign a user out of the Mega Financial client portal or cause a website notification to appear again.

8. Authenticated Client Acknowledgement

Mega Financial may maintain a protected record showing that an authenticated client acknowledged the current version of this notice. Updating this notice to a materially different version may require a new acknowledgement.

9. Changes to This Notice

Mega Financial may update this notice when website functionality, service providers, data practices, or applicable requirements change.
