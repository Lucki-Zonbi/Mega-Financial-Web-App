Company Story

Built to make tax season more organized and accessible.

Mega Financial is a Metro Detroit financial services firm based in Eastpointe, Michigan, providing tax preparation, accounting, bookkeeping, payroll, and business financial services for individuals, self-employed professionals, small businesses, and growing companies. We help clients manage their finances from filing and organizing to managing, planning, and growing. Our services are designed to provide practical financial support throughout the year—not just during tax season.

Mega Financial provides tax preparation and financial support services with a focus on clarity, convenience, and dependable client service. Through personalized assistance and secure online tools, we help clients stay organized and prepared throughout the tax preparation process.
