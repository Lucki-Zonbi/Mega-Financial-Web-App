Mega Financial Privacy Policy

Effective Version: September 16, 2026

Mega Financial respects the privacy of its clients and website users. This Privacy Policy explains the categories of information that may be collected through the Mega Financial website and client portal, why that information is used, how it may be stored or shared, and the safeguards associated with the information.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use Mega Financial services, information collected may include your name, email address, telephone number, account information, tax intake information, documents that you choose to provide, appointment information, payment-related transaction records, portal communications, and information necessary to maintain the security and operation of your account.

Tax and financial documents may contain additional personal, financial, employment, household, identification, or tax-related information supplied by you for the purpose of receiving Mega Financial services.

2. How Information Is Used

Mega Financial may use information to create and administer client accounts, provide requested tax and consulting services, process tax intake information, manage client documents, communicate with clients, coordinate appointments, administer payments, maintain portal security, prevent unauthorized access, troubleshoot services, maintain appropriate business records, and comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

3. Client Portal and Account Data

The Mega Financial client portal uses authenticated accounts to associate protected client activity with the appropriate client record. Clients are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their account credentials and should notify Mega Financial if they believe their account has been accessed without authorization.

4. Document Storage

Documents submitted through the protected client workflow may be stored using private cloud storage. Document metadata and related client records may be maintained separately in Mega Financial's application database. Access to protected documents is intended to be limited to authorized users and administrators acting within their permitted roles.

5. Service Providers

Mega Financial may use third-party technology and service providers to operate portions of its website, database, document storage, communications, scheduling, payment, hosting, and virtual-meeting services. Information may be processed by those providers when necessary to provide their services to Mega Financial.

Third-party services may maintain their own privacy policies and terms governing their systems.

6. Payments

Payment processing may be performed through a third-party payment processor. Mega Financial's application may maintain payment-related status and transaction information necessary to associate a payment with a client or service. Payment processors may separately collect and process payment credentials under their own privacy and security practices.

7. Cookies, Browser Storage and Technical Data

The Mega Financial website and client portal may use browser-based storage and technical information necessary to support authenticated sessions, user preferences, security, and application functionality. Third-party services used by Mega Financial may also use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

Additional information is available in the Mega Financial Cookie & Data Collection Notice.

8. Information Sharing

Mega Financial does not intend to sell client tax documents or protected client information. Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to provide requested services, work with authorized service providers, protect the security or integrity of Mega Financial systems, comply with legal obligations, respond to lawful requests, or protect applicable rights and interests.

9. Data Security

Mega Financial uses administrative, technical, and application safeguards intended to reduce unauthorized access to protected information. These safeguards may include authentication, authorization, password hashing, protected database access, private document storage, encryption provided by infrastructure services, request controls, and security logging.

No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security. Clients should therefore use strong, unique passwords and follow reasonable security practices when accessing their accounts.

10. Data Retention

Mega Financial may retain client information for the period reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain business and tax-service records, address security or dispute matters, and satisfy applicable legal or regulatory requirements. Different categories of records may require different retention periods.

11. Policy Acknowledgement Records

When an authenticated client acknowledges this Privacy Policy, Mega Financial may maintain a record identifying the client, policy version, acknowledgement date and time, and an associated acknowledgement record. This information is maintained to document which version of the policy was presented and acknowledged.

12. Policy Updates

Mega Financial may revise this Privacy Policy as its services, technology, or legal obligations change. Materially updated versions may be assigned a new effective version, and clients may be asked to acknowledge the updated policy.

13. Questions and Privacy Requests

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy or Mega Financial's handling of personal information should be directed to Mega Financial using its official client communication channels.
